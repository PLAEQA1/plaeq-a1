PLÄQ — Polyester Materials for a Closed Material Loop

PLÄQ is developing a new approach to polyester materials, aiming to combine industrial compatibility with more efficient chemical recycling.

Development Target Closed Loop ≥ 90% Target value only; the reference basis and assessment method will be defined in the testing protocol. Not yet experimentally validated.

01 / The Challenge

Circularity as an Industrial Challenge.

Polymer materials must meet functional requirements while becoming better integrated into circular material systems. Chemical recycling can be one component of closing material loops; its actual efficiency must be assessed using reliable data.

MARKET DYNAMICS

Economic and Regulatory Pressure

European plastics policy is increasing the importance of recycling and the circular economy. The specific implications of EU contributions and national levies require precise interpretation and legal review.

MATERIAL SYSTEMS

Thinking Beyond the First Life Cycle

A circular strategy also considers recovery, purification and the potential reuse of recovered feedstocks.

02 / Development Target

A Closed Material Loop as a Measurable Ambition.

PLÄQ has the long-term goal of reusing recovered feedstocks to manufacture polymer materials again. Closed Loop ≥ 90% is a development ambition, not an experimentally demonstrated result.

VISION

Recovery and Reuse

The intended loop considers recovery and renewed material production together. Its feasibility will be assessed against defined measurement and evaluation criteria.

EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIMS

Results Must Be Measurable

A robust assessment distinguishes polymer conversion, release, isolated recovery and the quality of the recovered substances.

03 / Research Approach

Methodological Rigor. Clear Evidence Boundaries.

PLÄQ follows a hypothesis-driven and traceable development approach. Claims about technical performance will only be made when supported by appropriate data.

Predefined Criteria

Evaluation criteria are intended to be established and documented before results are analyzed.

Transparent Evidence

Hypotheses, measurement data, interpretation and confirmed results are kept clearly distinct.

Traceable Decisions

Development steps are intended to be evaluated against documented criteria.

This page describes the project's methodological principles; it does not claim that all tests or experiments have already been carried out.

04 / Development Philosophy

Industrial Compatibility. Scientific Restraint.

We combine an industry-oriented development goal with careful assessment of its feasibility. We consistently distinguish between what we aim to achieve, what we are investigating and what the data actually demonstrate.

05 / Partnerships

Let's Advance Circular Material Systems Together.

We welcome discussions with research institutes, materials and recycling companies, and potential development partners. Technical details are not disclosed on this public page.
